# Hawa Aero — BLDC Smart Ceiling Fan

## Overview: Specifications
The Hawa Aero is a 1200 mm (48 inch) BLDC smart ceiling fan with 3 blades. It draws 32 W at
speed 5 and only 1.5 W in standby. It works on 140 V to 285 V AC, 50 Hz. Air delivery at
speed 5 is 230 CMM (cubic metres per minute). The fan has 5 speeds plus Boost and Sleep modes,
an IR remote control (model HR-10, needs 2 x AAA batteries) and built-in Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only)
for control from the Hawa Home app. Warranty details are in the general warranty policy.

## Speed Settings: Speeds 1 to 5
The Aero has 5 fixed speeds. Approximate motor speeds are:
- Speed 1: 110 RPM (about 6 W) — quiet, good for night
- Speed 2: 170 RPM (about 10 W)
- Speed 3: 230 RPM (about 16 W)
- Speed 4: 290 RPM (about 24 W)
- Speed 5: 350 RPM (about 32 W)
You can change the fan speed with the remote (buttons 1 to 5), with the Hawa Home app, or by
asking the Hawa assistant. The fan remembers the last speed after a power cut.

## Speed Settings: Boost and Sleep Modes
Boost mode runs the fan at 380 RPM, above speed 5, for a fixed 2 hours. After 2 hours the fan
goes back to the speed it was on before Boost. Press the "B" button on the remote to start or
stop Boost. Sleep mode (the moon button) lowers the speed by one step every 60 minutes until it
reaches speed 1, and the fan LED dims to 10% brightness. Sleep mode does not switch the fan off;
use the timer (1, 2, 4 or 8 hours) if you want the fan to stop on its own.

## Troubleshooting: Beeping
The Aero uses beeps to tell you what is happening:
- 1 short beep: command received.
- 2 short beeps: you pressed a speed button but the fan is already at that speed, or at the
  speed limit.
- 3 long beeps repeated every 10 seconds: input voltage is outside the safe range of
  140 V to 285 V. The fan stops to protect the motor and restarts by itself when voltage is normal.
- Continuous beeping: the motor is blocked or jammed (for example a blade is touching something).
  Switch the fan off at the wall switch, wait until the blades stop and check for obstruction.
  If it continues, raise a service ticket. Do not open the canopy or motor housing yourself.

## Troubleshooting: LED Indicator Codes
The status LED on the bottom of the fan shows:
- Solid blue: connected to Wi-Fi and the Hawa cloud.
- Fast blinking blue (2 times per second): Wi-Fi pairing mode is active (lasts 3 minutes).
- Slow blinking blue (once every 2 seconds): connected to Wi-Fi but cannot reach the Hawa cloud.
- Solid amber: not connected to Wi-Fi; the fan still works with the remote.
- Red blinking twice, then pause: motor overheat. The fan stops for 15 minutes to cool down and
  then restarts on speed 1.
- No light: no power, or the LED was switched off in the app (Settings > Night Light).

## Troubleshooting: Fan Offline in App
If the app shows the fan as "Offline":
1. Check the wall switch is on and the fan works with the remote.
2. Make sure your router is on 2.4 GHz. The Aero cannot connect to 5 GHz-only networks.
3. Check the LED colour (see LED indicator codes).
4. If you changed your Wi-Fi name or password, re-pair the fan from the app.
5. Move the router closer; the fan needs a signal of at least -70 dBm.
A fan that is offline can still be controlled with the IR remote, but not from the app or
the assistant.

## Reset: Factory Reset and Wi-Fi Reset
Wi-Fi reset (keeps your speed settings): press and hold the "Timer" button on the remote for
5 seconds. The LED starts fast blinking blue and the fan is ready to pair again.
Factory reset (erases everything, including Wi-Fi, timers and schedules): press and hold the
"Timer" and "B" (Boost) buttons together for 8 seconds. The fan beeps 4 times and the LED
blinks blue. Alternative without remote: switch the wall switch OFF and ON 5 times, keeping
it ON for about 2 seconds each time; the fan beeps 4 times when the reset is done.
After a factory reset, remove the fan from the Hawa Home app and add it again.

## Remote Control: Pairing and Batteries
The HR-10 remote uses 2 x AAA batteries (do not use the CR2032 coin cell from older Hawa remotes).
If the fan does not respond, replace the batteries first. To pair a new remote: switch the fan
on at the wall and, within 30 seconds, hold the "1" and "5" buttons together for 3 seconds.
The fan beeps once when the remote is paired. One fan can be paired with up to 2 remotes.

## Care: Cleaning and Maintenance
Switch off the fan at the wall before cleaning. Wipe the blades with a dry or slightly damp
microfibre cloth once a month. Do not use water sprays, petrol, thinner or harsh cleaners on the
blades or motor. The BLDC motor is sealed and needs no oiling. Never hang anything on the fan.
