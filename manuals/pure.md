# Hawa Pure RO — Water Purifier

## Overview: Specifications
The Hawa Pure RO is a wall-mounted RO + UV + Mineraliser water purifier with an 8 litre storage
tank. It purifies about 15 litres per hour. It is suitable for input water with TDS up to
2000 ppm and inlet water pressure between 0.4 and 4 kg/cm2. Power use is 36 W while purifying.
Water recovery is up to 60%, which means about 6 litres of pure water for every 10 litres of
input water. The rejected water can be used for mopping or plants.

## Filters: Filter Life and Replacement Schedule
The Pure RO has 4 replaceable parts. Replace each one at the time or volume below, whichever
comes first:
- Sediment filter: every 6 months or 3,000 litres
- Activated carbon filter: every 12 months or 6,000 litres
- RO membrane: every 24 months or 8,000 litres
- UV lamp: every 12 months
The app shows remaining filter life as a percentage. The FILTER LED on the purifier is green
above 10%, turns amber at 10% or below, and turns red at 0%. At 0% the purifier stops
dispensing water to protect water quality, until the filter is replaced.

## Filters: How to Replace Filters
Use only genuine Hawa filters. The filter service kit is HP-FK-02 (sediment + carbon) and the
membrane kit is HP-MB-08. Using non-genuine filters voids the warranty.
1. Switch off the purifier and close the inlet water valve.
2. Open the front cover and twist the old filter anticlockwise to remove it.
3. Insert the new filter and twist clockwise until it clicks.
4. Open the inlet valve and switch the purifier on.
5. Reset the filter counter: press and hold the FILTER button for 5 seconds until you hear
   2 beeps (or use the app: Pure RO > Filters > Reset).
6. Drain the first 2 full tanks of water after a filter change; do not drink it.
RO membrane and UV lamp replacement must be done by a Hawa technician.

## Water Quality: TDS and Taste
TDS (total dissolved solids) of the output water is shown on the display. The Mineraliser keeps
output TDS between 50 and 150 ppm; the factory setting is 80 ppm. You can adjust it in the app
(Pure RO > Water Taste) within that range. If output TDS stays above 150 ppm for more than
24 hours, the membrane may be worn out; raise a service ticket.
The Hawa Pure RO is not a medical device. It improves drinking water quality but Hawa makes no
claim that it treats, cures or prevents any disease. For health questions, please consult a
doctor.

## Troubleshooting: Error Codes
- E1: low inlet water pressure (below 0.4 kg/cm2). Check the inlet valve and water supply.
- E2: tank full sensor fault. Switch off for 1 minute and on again; if it repeats, raise a ticket.
- E3: UV lamp failure. The purifier stops dispensing until the UV lamp is replaced by a technician.
- E4: leakage detected. The purifier closes the inlet valve automatically. Switch it off, wipe
  the area dry and raise a service ticket.
- E5: filter life 0%. Replace the filter and reset the counter.

## Care: Cleaning the Storage Tank
Clean the 8 litre storage tank every 3 months. Switch off the purifier, drain all water using
the dispense button, wipe the inside of the tank with a clean soft cloth and food-grade
cleaning solution, rinse twice with purified water and switch on again. If the purifier is not
used for more than 7 days (for example during travel), drain the tank and discard the first
full tank after you return.
