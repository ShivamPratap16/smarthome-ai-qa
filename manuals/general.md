# Hawa Home — General Policies

## Warranty: Coverage Periods
Warranty starts from the date on the purchase invoice:
- Hawa Aero ceiling fan: 3 years on the whole fan, and 5 years on the BLDC motor.
- Hawa Lock S1: 2 years on electronics and 5 years on the mechanical lock body.
- Hawa Pure RO: 1 year on the purifier. Filters, the RO membrane and the UV lamp are
  consumables and are not covered by warranty.
Register your product in the Hawa Home app within 30 days of purchase to get 6 extra months of
warranty (one time per product). You can check your warranty with your product serial number,
for example HA-AERO-1001. The serial number is printed on the box and on a sticker on the product.

## Warranty: What Voids the Warranty
The warranty does not cover, and becomes void in case of:
- Opening the fan motor housing, the sealed lock electronics, or the purifier's sealed parts.
- Installation or repair by anyone who is not a Hawa certified technician.
- Damage from voltage outside the rated range, lightning, fire, flood or accidents.
- Using non-genuine filters or spare parts.
- Physical damage, rust caused by misuse, or a removed or changed serial number sticker.
Normal wear of remote buttons and the cosmetic finish is not covered.

## Service: Tickets and Visit Times
You can raise a service ticket in the Hawa Home app, by asking the Hawa assistant, or by calling
support. A ticket needs the device, a short description of the issue and a mobile number.
Ticket numbers look like HW-100001. A technician visits within 48 hours in metro cities and
within 72 hours in other cities. Visits under warranty are free. Out of warranty, the visit
charge is Rs 299 plus the cost of parts. Service hours are 9 AM to 9 PM, all 7 days.
Replacement: if a product has a manufacturing defect within 7 days of delivery, Hawa replaces it.

## App: Pairing Over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
Download the Hawa Home app (Android 9+ or iOS 15+) and sign in with your mobile number.
- Hawa Aero and Hawa Pure RO connect over Wi-Fi. Only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is supported, not 5 GHz.
  Put the device in pairing mode, tap "Add device" in the app and follow the steps.
- Hawa Lock S1 connects over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) from up to 10 metres. For remote
  control from outside the home, add the Hawa Bridge, which connects the lock to Wi-Fi.
A device can belong to only one home at a time. Up to 10 family members can share a home.

## Safety: Electrical Wiring
For your safety, never open the fan canopy, junction box, switchboard, lock panel wiring or
purifier electrical parts yourself, and never change or repair any wiring. Mains electricity
can cause serious injury or death. Always switch off power and contact a Hawa certified
technician by raising a service ticket. The Hawa assistant does not give do-it-yourself
instructions for electrical wiring work.

## Contact: Support and Privacy Policy
Customer support: toll-free 1800-000-4292 (9 AM to 9 PM, all days) or email
care@hawahome.example. Hawa will never ask for your OTP, passwords, bank details or Aadhaar
number. Share your mobile number only to raise a service ticket. Chat logs are stored with
phone numbers and email addresses masked, and are deleted after 90 days.
