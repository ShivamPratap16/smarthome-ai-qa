# Hawa Lock S1 — Smart Door Lock

## Overview: Specifications and Unlock Methods
The Hawa Lock S1 is a smart door lock for doors 35 mm to 90 mm thick. It can be unlocked in
5 ways: fingerprint, PIN code, RFID card, the Hawa Home app (Bluetooth, or Wi-Fi via the Hawa
Bridge), and a mechanical backup key. It stores up to 100 fingerprints, 50 PIN codes
(each 6 to 10 digits) and 50 RFID cards. The outer panel is rated IP54 (splash resistant).
The tamper alarm sounds at 90 dB.

## Batteries: Low Battery Warning
The Lock S1 runs on 8 x AA alkaline batteries (1.5 V). Typical battery life is about 10 months
with 10 unlocks a day. Do not use rechargeable 1.2 V batteries; the lock will show low battery
too early.
- At 20% battery: the lock beeps 3 times after every unlock and the keypad shows a red battery
  icon. The app sends a "Low battery" notification.
- At 10% battery: the lock still works for about 50 more unlocks. Replace batteries now.
- At 5% battery: fingerprint and app unlock are disabled to save power; only PIN, card and
  mechanical key work.
Replace all 8 batteries at the same time. Your users and settings are not lost when you
change batteries.

## Batteries: Emergency Power
If the batteries are completely dead and you are locked out, connect a power bank with a USB-C
cable to the emergency USB-C port at the bottom of the outer panel. The lock wakes up in about
10 seconds and you can unlock it with your own fingerprint or PIN. After entering, replace the
batteries immediately. You can also use the mechanical backup key behind the logo cover.

## Users: Fingerprint Enrolment
The first fingerprint you add becomes the Admin fingerprint. Only an admin can add or delete
other users.
Using the app: open Hawa Home > Lock S1 > Users > Add user > Fingerprint, then place the same
finger on the sensor 6 times, lifting it between each touch, until the app shows "Enrolled".
Using the keypad: press * then #, enter the admin PIN, press 1 (Add user) then 1 (Fingerprint),
and place the finger 6 times. The lock gives 2 short beeps when enrolment is successful.
Tips: enrol at least 2 fingers per person, keep fingers dry and clean, and enrol children's
fingers again every 6 months because fingerprints change as they grow.

## Security: Auto-lock and Wrong Attempts
Auto-lock is on by default and locks the door 10 seconds after it closes. You can change the
delay between 5 and 60 seconds in the app (Lock S1 > Settings > Auto-lock).
After 5 wrong fingerprint, PIN or card attempts in a row, the keypad is frozen for 3 minutes
and the lock sounds an alarm. The app also sends a security alert to the admin. Use a
"scramble PIN" (extra random digits before or after your real PIN, up to 16 digits total)
to hide your PIN from people watching.
For your safety, Hawa support and the Hawa assistant never help anyone bypass, force open or
unlock a lock without the owner's own fingerprint, PIN, card, app or key.

## Reset: Factory Reset
A factory reset deletes all fingerprints, PINs, cards and the admin. It can only be done from
the inside of the door, so only someone already inside can reset the lock. Open the battery
cover on the inner panel, press and hold the small reset button with a pin for 5 seconds until
you hear a long beep, then enter 000# on the keypad. Remove the lock from the app and pair it
again afterwards.
